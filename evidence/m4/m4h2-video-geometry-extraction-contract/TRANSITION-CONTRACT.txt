M4H-A transitional integration

Historical callable surfaces remain available during this subtranche.

screen_fit_geometry_valid_for_mode()
    Becomes a thin adapter to:
        pstvnc_video_geometry_safe_area_valid()

screen_fit_geometry_valid()
    Remains the active_video_mode adapter.
    It does not move to permanent geometry.

calculate_safe_desktop_geometry()
    Remains a compatibility/runtime adapter.
    It reads the current screen-fit state.
    For FULL/bypass it preserves current full-raster behavior.
    Otherwise it calls:
        pstvnc_video_geometry_safe_area_rect()

calculate_presented_desktop_geometry()
    Remains a compatibility/runtime adapter.
    It obtains current safe geometry and calls:
        pstvnc_video_geometry_presented_rect()
    with the existing VNC capacity.

ps2vnc_config_presented_geometry_for_mode()
    Remains owned by config/management as an adapter.
    It converts config fields to geometry inputs and delegates the shared
    rectangle arithmetic to the permanent geometry API.

screen_fit_apply_preset()
    Does NOT move.
    It mutates calibration state and retains fallback policy.

screen_fit_clamp_interactive()
    Does NOT move in M4H-A.
    It remains calibration-interaction policy.

region_fits_u32()
    Does NOT become a public geometry dependency in M4H-A.
    geometry.c may use its own private overflow-safe rectangle bounds
    helper so the permanent module remains self-contained.

No mutable global state moves in M4H-A.
