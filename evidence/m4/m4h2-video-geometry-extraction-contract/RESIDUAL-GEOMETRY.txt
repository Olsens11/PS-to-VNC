M4H residual geometry after M4H-A

M4H-A is intentionally not the whole video/geometry tranche.

A later residual audit must classify mode-derived helpers currently tied
to active_video_mode / active_display_backend, including areas such as:

    HIRES effective width
    HIRES full-frame/storage height
    horizontal raster mapping requirement
    vertical raster mapping requirement
    raster mapping requirement
    logical desktop size
    other mode-derived mapping calculations

Those helpers may become M4H-B if they are pure geometry after explicit
mode/backend parameterization.

Framebuffer copying/publishing itself does not move merely because it
uses geometry values.

PS2 GS programming does not move.

Mutable desktop state does not move.

Completion of M4H-A therefore does NOT by itself mark video/geometry
fully normalized.
